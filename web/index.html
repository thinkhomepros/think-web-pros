Frequently asked questions

What is GEO, and why does my small business website need it?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization. More people now ask AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews for a business recommendation instead of scrolling search results. GEO means your site states who you are, what you offer, where you are and how to reach you in a clear, structured way those assistants can read and quote. Think Web Pros sites are built for both classic SEO and GEO.

Do I need design or coding skills?

No. You click any words on the page to type over them, tap a photo to upload your own, and pick a style from the Design panel. There is no code and nothing to install.

How much does a Think Web Pros website cost?

Designing is free. When you're happy with your site, plans start at a one-time $299 design fee plus $19 per month for Starter. Growth is $599 plus $39 per month and Pro is $1,199 plus $79 per month. Hosting and SSL are included in every plan. For more elaborate designs, our Custom plan is priced per job: request a quote and we'll reply with a price.

How long do you keep my design?

Without a plan, we keep a design for 1 hour from when you start it. Saving keeps it current but does not add time. When you choose a plan, your designs are kept for as long as you have it. If you're not ready to choose, Design storage keeps all your designs for $20 a month.

Can I use my own logo, or make one if I don't have one?

Yes to both. Upload the logo you already have, or describe what you want to the AI logo maker. Your first 3 AI requests are free, and the AI logo package for unlimited changes is $49 once.

Can I build and edit my website from my phone?

Yes. The Think Web Pros Studio works on phones, tablets and computers, and you can preview how your site looks on each one while you edit.

What web services can I add?

You can add a custom domain and email, online booking, an online store, live chat, an SEO boost with Google Business setup, professional copywriting, a photo shoot, and a point of sale system for in-person sales that syncs with your website store.
